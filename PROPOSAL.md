## Client Brief

**Client:** Kayla, founder of Kayla & Bloom — a student-run floral studio (University of Michigan) selling bouquets, dorm bedside arrangements, and custom/event florals, with plans to grow into weddings.

**Purpose:** Give Kayla a site that shows her menu and pricing, and lets people request an order or event without needing a phone call.

**Audience:** Primarily college students and sorority houses — think standing orders for a chapter house, a bedside bouquet before finals, a bouquet for a friend — plus some off-campus customers wanting recurring home arrangements.

**Key action:** Submit an inquiry (name, contact info, what they want, date needed) so Kayla can follow up directly. No checkout yet — she confirms details and payment (Venmo) after the fact.

**Pages needed:**
- Home — intro, brand feel, link into the menu
- Menu & Subscriptions — bouquet sizes/pricing, add-ons, monthly Bloom Subscriptions
- Custom Creations — sorority/event/custom orders, how ordering works, care tips
- Contact — the inquiry form

**Content status:** Have — brand name and logo mark, full pricing for every bouquet size and subscription tier, add-on pricing, brand color palette (blush/cream, rose accents) and font pairing (Playfair Display + Quicksand), and brand illustration assets (swans, florals). Still need — real photos of Kayla's actual arrangements (current images are illustrated brand motifs, not product photos); Kayla has not yet reviewed this rebuilt version page-by-page.

**Style preferences:** "Girly but classy" — soft pink and white/cream, romantic and delicate, not childish. Confirmed via the logo and brand mockup Kayla had already approved: warm cream background, rose/mauve text, serif display headings paired with a soft rounded sans-serif.

**Inspiration sites:** Not collected as part of this interview — the call was a scoping conversation for an existing paid engagement (Kayla & Bloom is a client of Ivy Devs, my own web dev business, and my professor approved using this case for the assignment), not a from-scratch client interview, so the standard "show 3–5 competitor sites" step didn't happen. **Still needed:** a quick follow-up with Kayla — show her a few other student/small-business florist sites and note what she likes/dislikes, to fill this in honestly rather than guessing.
